1. Overview & Vision
Weda.lk is a trusted, nationwide platform connecting residents and businesses across Sri Lanka with reliable, vetted professionals for both skilled trades and home help services. The Phase 1 website will serve as the primary digital platform to build a national brand, acquire users from multiple provinces, and facilitate job requests through a centrally managed, semi-automated model.
The long-term vision is to become Sri Lanka's leading and most trusted platform for all home-based services.
Strategic Note on MVP Philosophy: A nationwide launch from day one significantly increases complexity compared to a local pilot. While we will still manage many backend processes manually, the platform must have a robust location-based architecture from the start to be functional. This is a more ambitious and resource-intensive Phase 1.
2. The Problem
For Customers (Service Takers): Across Sri Lanka, it is difficult, risky, and time-consuming to find trustworthy individuals for both urgent repairs and ongoing domestic help. There is no single, trusted national platform that properly vets professionals.
For Professionals (Service Providers): Skilled tradespeople and domestic workers across the country struggle with inconsistent work and lack the means to connect with a broad, national market of reliable employers.
3. Target Audience (User Personas)
Persona 1: The Customer ("Anusha")
Who: A 35-year-old professional living with her family in any major Sri Lankan city (e.g., Kandy, Galle, Colombo).
Pain Points: Her A/C is broken, and she is looking for a reliable part-time cook. She is concerned about safety and trustworthiness, regardless of her location.
Goal: To find a reliable, vetted professional through a single, trusted platform that works in her city.
Persona 2 & 3: The Professionals ("Saman" & "Nirmala")
Who: Skilled professionals located anywhere in Sri Lanka.
Goal: To connect with a larger market of customers beyond their immediate neighborhood and get consistent, safe work opportunities.
4. Strategic Goals for Phase 1
Establish a Trusted National Brand: The website and our vetting process must project the highest level of safety and reliability nationwide.
Solve the "Chicken & Egg" Problem: Successfully build a base of both customers and professionals in at least 3 key provinces (e.g., Western, Central, Southern).
Build a National Database: Successfully register at least 200 Customers and 75 Vetted Professionals from multiple provinces.
Facilitate Jobs Nationally: Manually facilitate at least 100 paid jobs/placements across the active provinces.
5. Website Structure & Core Features
5.1 Public-Facing Pages (No Login Required)
Homepage:
Hero Section: Headline: "Sri Lanka's Trusted Partner for Home Services." A prominent location search bar ("Enter your city to get started") is now the primary Call-to-Action.
Our Service Categories: Skilled Trades and Home Help.
The Weda.lk Guarantee (Trust Section): Emphasize the rigorous, standardized vetting process applied to all professionals, regardless of location.
"Become a Pro" Page:
Clear sections for both "Skilled Trades" and "Home Help," outlining the national requirements.
5.2 User Accounts & Registration
Unified Registration Page:
Fields: Full Name, Email, Password, Mobile Number.
Primary City/District (Mandatory, searchable dropdown of major cities/districts in Sri Lanka).
Radio Button: "I am a Customer" OR "I am a Professional."
5.3 Customer (Service Taker) Account & Flow
The Job Request Flow:
Form Fields:
Service Category & Specific Service.
Job Type (One-Time/Ongoing).
Describe your need.
Preferred Date/Start Date & Time.
Address.
City/District (Mandatory, auto-filled from profile but editable). This is the key field for matching.
Backend/Frontend Action: The request is logged in the central system and tagged with its location. An alert is sent to you (the admin) for manual processing.
5.4 Professional (Service Provider) Account & Flow
Professional Dashboard:
Profile Fields:
Profile Picture, Primary Skill, Experience, Bio.
Service Category (Skilled Trade/Home Help).
Service Locations (CRITICAL FEATURE: A multi-select list of cities/districts where they are willing to work).
Status indicator: Profile Status: "Pending Vetting".
The Vetting & Onboarding Flow (CRITICAL):
Manual Admin Work: Your vetting process must now support remote verification.
Standard Vetting (All Pros): Digital verification of NIC, phone-based reference checks.
High-Security Vetting (Home Help): Require professionals to digitally submit scans of their mandatory police clearance certificate and a certified letter from their Grama Niladhari (GN). You will need a process to verify the authenticity of these documents.
6. Risk Mitigation & Management
The Marketplace Problem: A nationwide launch creates a "chicken & egg" problem in every city. We will mitigate this by launching marketing campaigns for both customers and professionals simultaneously in our target provinces.
Remote Vetting Challenge: Vetting professionals without meeting them in person is a major risk. Our primary mitigation is the strict requirement for official, verifiable documents (Police & GN reports) for all Home Help staff. We may need to partner with regional agents in the future to handle in-person checks.
Operational Overload: A single person cannot manage a nationwide concierge service. The model assumes the hiring of at least one administrative assistant to help manage job requests from day one.
7. Out of Scope for Phase 1
The following remain out of scope to manage complexity, but the pressure to build them will be higher:
Fully automated matching, scheduling, and payments.
In-app chat.
A public, searchable directory of professionals.
8. Success Metrics
Achieving user and professional registrations in at least 3 provinces.
Conversion Rate: (Completed jobs / Submitted requests) remains above 70%.
Successful completion of at least 20 placements in the high-risk "Home Help" category across different cities, with positive feedback.
Time-to-match: Average time taken to connect a customer with a professional remains under 4 hours.

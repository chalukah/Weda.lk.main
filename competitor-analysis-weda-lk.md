# Competitor Analysis: Weda.lk

_Nationwide Sri Lankan Platform for Home Services_
_Date: August 11, 2025_

---

## Section 1: Analysis Scope

**Business Model**: Two-sided marketplace connecting customers with vetted professionals
**Geographic Market**: Nationwide Sri Lanka (all provinces)
**Service Categories**:

- Skilled Trades (repairs, maintenance, technical services)
- Home Help (domestic services, personal assistance)

**Key Differentiators**:

- Rigorous vetting process (including police clearance for home help)
- Nationwide coverage from launch
- Focus on trust and safety
- Semi-automated centralized management model

## Section 3: Detailed Competitor Analysis

### 3.1 QuickHelp.lk - Deep Dive Analysis

_Primary Direct Competitor (Currently Suspended)_

**Company Overview:**

- **Founded**: 2019
- **Status**: Paused B2C operations effective July 1, 2025
- **Funding**: Rs. 21 million seed round (backed by Viridian Fund, Lankan Angel Network's Angel Fund, A1M Capital)
- **Recognition**: Aavishkaar Impact Award at Venture Engine 2021/2022

**Business Model Analysis:**

- **Revenue Model**: Commission-based marketplace (specific rates not disclosed)
- **Service Delivery**: Managed marketplace with vetted professionals
- **Coverage**: Limited to Colombo 1-15, Dehiwala-Mount Lavinia, Nugegoda-Nawala, Rajagiriya-Battaramulla
- **Scale**: 5,000+ customers, 14,000+ jobs completed, 4.7/5 service rating

**Services Offered:**

- Home Cleaning (deep cleaning, regular maintenance)
- Appliance Repairs (AC, washing machine, refrigerator, TV)
- Professional Services (plumbing, electrical work, painting)
- Specialized Services (gardening, carpentry, masonry, handyman)

**Key Features:**

- Background-checked service providers with domain knowledge testing
- 24/7 customer support during bookings
- Same-day service availability
- "No extra charges" pricing model
- Mobile app and web platform

**Pricing Strategy:**

- Transparent upfront pricing
- No hidden fees or extra charges
- Premium pricing positioning for quality assurance

**Market Positioning:**

- "Reliable care for your home at your fingertips"
- Focus on trust through background verification
- Professional service quality over price competition

**Strengths:**

- Strong funding and investor backing
- Proven demand with significant transaction volume
- Rigorous vetting process builds trust
- Professional brand positioning
- Strong customer satisfaction ratings

**Weaknesses:**

- Limited geographic coverage (Colombo area only)
- Currently suspended operations (major market exit)
- High operational costs likely contributed to suspension
- Limited scalability model evidenced by operational pause

**Current Status Impact:**

- Creates immediate market opportunity for Weda.lk
- Demonstrates both demand and operational challenges in the market
- Potential for talent and customer acquisition from suspended competitor
- Market education already completed by QuickHelp

### 3.2 ikman.lk - Market Leader Analysis

_Largest Marketplace Platform (Indirect Competitor)_

**Company Overview:**

- **Position**: Sri Lanka's largest online marketplace
- **Business Model**: Classified ads platform with services section
- **Coverage**: Nationwide Sri Lanka
- **Scale**: Dominant market position in online classifieds

**Services Marketplace Section:**

- Domestic services listings
- Skilled trades (electricians, plumbers, carpenters)
- Business and industrial services
- Property maintenance services

**Business Model Analysis:**

- **Revenue Model**: Membership fees and premium listing charges
- **Service Delivery**: Classified listings model (not managed marketplace)
- **Customer Experience**: Self-service connection between buyers and service providers

**Membership Pricing Structure:**

- **Free Basic**: Limited ad postings with basic visibility
- **Business Plus**: Increased ad limits, enhanced features
- **Business Unlimited**: Maximum posting allowance, premium positioning
- **Premium Benefits**: 20 images per ad, premium member badge, dedicated business page
- **Additional Services**: Bump Up promotions, Top Ad placements, ikman delivers integration

**Key Features:**

- Massive user base and brand recognition
- Multi-category marketplace reduces customer acquisition costs
- Mobile app and web platform
- Established payment systems
- Geographic coverage across Sri Lanka

**Market Positioning:**

- "Buy, Rent, Find or Sell Anything"
- Focus on comprehensive marketplace rather than specialized services
- Price-competitive environment

**Strengths:**

- Largest user base in Sri Lankan online marketplace
- Strong brand recognition and trust
- Nationwide coverage
- Established infrastructure and payment systems
- Cross-category customer acquisition benefits

**Weaknesses:**

- Not a managed marketplace (no service quality assurance)
- Classified ads model lacks professional service coordination
- No standardized pricing or service delivery
- Quality control limited to user ratings and reviews
- Competing priorities across multiple categories

**Competitive Analysis vs Weda.lk:**

- **User Base**: ikman wins on existing user volume
- **Service Quality**: Weda.lk advantage through managed marketplace
- **Trust & Safety**: Weda.lk advantage through professional vetting
- **Geographic Coverage**: Both offer nationwide reach
- **Service Categories**: Similar service offerings

## Section 4: Market Analysis

### 4.1 Market Size and Opportunity Assessment

**Sri Lankan Home Services Market Overview (2024-2025):**

**Economic Context:**

- **GDP**: Approximately $99 billion (2024)
- **Economic Growth**: 5.0% recovery in 2024 after 2022 crisis
- **Labor Force**: 8.59 million people
- **Employment Challenges**: 45.5% employment ratio, skills shortages persist

**Digital Market Infrastructure:**

- **Internet Penetration**: 53.6% (12.4 million users)
- **Mobile Penetration**: 115.1 per 100 inhabitants
- **E-commerce Market**: $4.61 billion transaction value (2024)
- **Digital Economy**: Contributes 5%+ to GDP
- **E-commerce Growth**: 20.2% annual growth rate

**Total Addressable Market (TAM) Estimation:**

- **Total Households**: ~5.5 million households (based on 22M population, 4 people per household average)
- **Urban Households**: ~1.0 million households (18.2% urban population)
- **Digitally Connected Households**: ~2.95 million households (53.6% internet penetration)
- **E-commerce Active Users**: 5.5 million users (37.8% penetration)

**Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM):**

- **Target Provinces**: Western, Central, Southern (covering ~60% of population = 13.2M people)
- **Target Households**: ~3.3 million households in target provinces
- **Internet-Connected Target Households**: ~1.77 million households
- **Estimated Service-Purchasing Households**: ~530,000 households (30% of connected households likely to use home services)

**Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) - Phase 1:**

- **Conservative Market Share Target**: 0.1% of SAM in Year 1
- **Target Customer Base**: 200 customers (aligns with startup brief goals)
- **Revenue Estimation**: $50,000-100,000 annually (based on average transaction values)

### 4.2 Market Trends and Growth Drivers

**Positive Growth Drivers:**

**1. Digital Adoption Acceleration:**

- Social media users increased 9.3% (700,000 new users) in 2024
- Mobile-first approach with 93.3% broadband mobile connections
- Growing comfort with digital payments and online services

**2. Urban Lifestyle Changes:**

- 18.2% urban population with higher service demand
- Dual-income households requiring domestic help
- Time-pressed professionals in major cities

**3. Post-COVID Service Demand:**

- Increased focus on home maintenance and cleanliness
- Remote work creating demand for home office services
- Health and safety consciousness driving professional service demand

**4. Economic Recovery Trends:**

- 5% economic growth in 2024 indicating recovery
- Tourism sector rebound creating service economy opportunities
- Construction sector growth driving skilled trades demand

**Market Headwinds:**

**1. Economic Constraints:**

- Household budgets remain stretched from 2022 crisis
- Real wages contracted 16.9-22% between 2021-2024
- Private consumption remains weak
- Poverty rate at 24.5% (double 2019 levels)

**2. Labor Market Challenges:**

- Brain drain: skilled workers emigrating for opportunities
- Skills mismatch between supply and demand
- Labor shortages in technical and skilled trades
- 92% literacy rate but limited market-relevant skills

**3. Trust and Safety Concerns:**

- Limited formal vetting mechanisms in existing market
- High informal economy participation
- Safety concerns for domestic help services
